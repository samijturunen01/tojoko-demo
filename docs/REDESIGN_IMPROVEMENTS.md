# Digital presence analysis and improvements – Tojoko Oy

Tojoko Oy has **no existing website**, so there was no site to audit. This is an analysis of the company's
current digital presence (checked 24.11.2026) and what the new site changes.

## Current presence

| Channel | State | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| Website | None | Not findable for service searches; nothing to send to new customers |
| Google Business Profile | Name, category, phone, hours, 6 reviews (5,0), no website link, no photos found | Good reputation but thin information; no services, no prices, no story |
| Fonecta / Finder | "Vahvistamaton profiili", no phone, no description | Directory visitors see no contact details |
| Social media | None found | — |
| Logo / brand | None found | No recognisable identity |

Competitors in Joensuu (Auto-France, HeisCar, Eronen, BoKo-Motors) all have websites with service lists,
and two offer online booking – Tojoko currently competes only through Google Maps and word of mouth.

## Most commercially meaningful improvements

1. **Be findable for intent searches** – prerendered pages for autohuolto / autokorjaukset / vianselvitys in
   Joensuu with unique titles, descriptions and `AutoRepair` structured data (verified facts only).
2. **Turn the strongest asset into a headline** – the reviews' recurring theme (persistence with hard faults)
   becomes the positioning: "Myös ne viat, joiden syy ei löydy helpolla."
3. **Contact without friction** – phone in the header, sticky mobile call bar, open-now indicator, and a
   24/7 time request that captures plate, symptom and timing.
4. **Better-qualified requests** – symptom picker and per-service "Kerro nämä" checklists.
5. **Trust without fabrication** – verified rating + count with date, summarised themes, link to Google;
   Y-tunnus, founding year and address visible.
6. **A brand** – original TOJOKO wordmark proposal (three socket O's, the "OK" inside the name) and the
   "Varjotaulu" visual identity.

## Recommended next steps outside the website

- Add the website link, services, photos and a description to the Google Business Profile.
- Verify the Fonecta profile and add the phone number.
- Ask satisfied customers for Google reviews (link: `company.google.writeReviewUrl`) – no incentives.
