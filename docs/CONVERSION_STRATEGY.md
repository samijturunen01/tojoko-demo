# Conversion strategy – Tojoko Oy

Verified facts: CONTENT_SOURCES.md. Everything marked *assumption* is a strategic assumption, not a fact.

## Business model

Small independent car repair shop (registry headcount 1) at Aspitie 16, Joensuu. Revenue comes from servicing,
repairs and troubleshooting booked by phone (*assumption:* mostly private car owners, some small-business
vehicles). Every job starts with a conversation: no fixed-price online booking.

## Customer segments

| Segment | Need / pain | Decision criteria | Preferred contact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Car owner with a fault or warning light | Uncertainty, worry about cost, "nobody finds the fault" | Competence, honesty, availability | Phone |
| Car owner needing routine service | Convenience, price, warranty worries at independent garages | Price, trust, ease of booking | Phone or form |
| Car failed inspection | Deadline for re-inspection | Speed, clarity | Phone |
| Comparing garages (research) | Can't evaluate a garage without a website | Reviews, location, clear info | Browsing → call later |
| Returning customer | Quick access to number and hours | — | Phone |

## Verified strengths

- Google rating 5,0 (6 reviews); friendliness mentioned in every written review
- Persistence with difficult faults (review), technical know-how (reviews), "regular garage" (2 reviews)
- Location in the Salpakangas business area, open weekdays 8–17
- Operating since 2020

## Goals

- **Primary conversion:** contact to agree a time – a phone call **or** an "aikapyyntö" (time request form).
  Phone is fastest; the form works 24/7 and collects the plate number and symptom description.
- **Secondary:** service page views, symptom-picker use, Google reviews click, directions click, map load.

## Customer journey

1. Search "autokorjaamo Joensuu" / "vikavalo palaa" / brand + Joensuu → lands on homepage or service page
2. Understands in seconds: car servicing + repair in Joensuu, also hard faults (H1 + subline)
3. Trust: 5,0 Google rating next to the CTA, review themes, Y-tunnus, address, hours, "open now"
4. Self-qualification: symptom picker tells what to tell the garage; service pages answer price/warranty/brand questions
5. Action: call (header, sticky mobile bar, every section) or the pre-filled request form
6. Directions: map + Google Maps links on the contact page

## Conversion barriers (no website) and responses

| Barrier | Response in the demo |
|---|---|
| No website – the garage is invisible outside Google Maps | Complete multi-page site with local SEO for Joensuu searches |
| Unknown services | Three clear service pages (only claims supported by sources) |
| Price uncertainty | Honest "what affects the price" + invitation to ask for an estimate; no invented prices |
| Hard to judge competence | Rating + review themes + "Lue arviot Googlessa" |
| Calling during work hours may not be convenient | 24/7 time request with pre-selected topic and symptom |
| Poorly described problems waste time | Symptom picker + "Kerro nämä" checklists → better-qualified requests |
| Warranty worry at independent garages | FAQ explaining EU rules (general information) |

## Hypotheses (to measure, not promises)

- Showing the 5,0 rating next to the primary CTA increases calls from first-time visitors.
- The symptom picker increases form completion and the share of requests with a usable description.
- A sticky mobile call bar increases phone clicks on mobile.
- A dedicated "Vianselvitys" page attracts high-intent searches ("vikavalo", "vika ei löydy").

## Measurement

See CONVERSION_MEASUREMENT.md. Success = qualified calls/requests that become booked jobs – not clicks.
