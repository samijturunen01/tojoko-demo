### Review themes (summarised, not quoted)

Of the 4 written Google reviews (checked 24.11.2026): all 4 mention friendly service; 2 praise technical
know-how; 1 praises persistence with faults other garages could not solve; 2 describe Tojoko as their regular
garage ("vakipaikka"). The site summarises these themes and links to Google – **review texts are not
republished** and no rating markup is added to structured data.

## Requires customer confirmation

1. **Services in detail.** The site only claims servicing, repairs and troubleshooting. Confirm: which makes /
   vehicle types (cars, vans, EV/hybrid?), tyres, A/C, electrical work, inspection repairs, service-book stamps,
   timing belts, etc. – then service pages can be made more specific.
2. **Email address** – none found. Add one if available (contact page + structured data).
3. **Owner / mechanic name and photo** – a personal introduction would be a strong trust signal.
4. **Price information** – any fixed prices (e.g. basic service, hourly rate) the owner wants to publish.
5. **Holiday hours** and whether hours on Google are current.
6. **Logo** – no authentic logo found; the TOJOKO socket wordmark is a Hades Design proposal.
7. **Process details** – callback practice for form requests, estimates before work, payment methods,
   courtesy car, key drop – none are claimed on the site until confirmed.
8. **Service area** – the site says Joensuu only.
9. **Warranty FAQ** – the EU block-exemption explanation is general consumer information; have it reviewed.
