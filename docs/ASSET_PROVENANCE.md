# Asset provenance – Tojoko Oy demo

Authorisation: the project owner (Hades Design) has confirmed permission to use and publish Tojoko Oy's
authentic logo, branding and authorised company photographs for this demo. **None were found** (no website,
no social media, no photos on the Google Business Profile), so the demo uses original graphics and
licensed stock photography only. Originals are in `source-assets/`; published variants are generated by
`npm run images` (`scripts/process-images.mjs`).
